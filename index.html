Your details

Camera and options

A recording is kept temporarily for analysis until you leave the session. To download the video, enable both video and session file saving.

Start from your exercise position. Keep your head and hand in the picture, raise one hand above your head and hold it for two seconds. A five-second on-screen countdown gives you time to lower your hand before recording starts. Raise a hand for three seconds to finish.

Spoken guidance uses Marin, an AI-generated voice with a soft, warm British female delivery. The countdown stays visible if audio is unavailable.

MediaPipe detects the hand gesture on this device. Camera access is needed; the microphone is not used.

Two ways to count on this device. Movement in the box counts from how the changed pixels move. Following the knee follows points on the knee from frame to frame, counting one repetition for each full swing. Movement in the box is also counted as a cross-check, with both counts saved for your physiotherapist.

Your programme, week by week

We are focusing first on heel slides, straight leg raises and seated knee extension, with accurate repetition counting and assessment of movement quality as our development priorities. After recording, select Analyse exercise for estimated angles, movement range, timings and clinical references. Camera measurements and provisional SLR estimates are not validated clinical scores. Other exercises are shown as pending and will be introduced in due course. The week you are in now is marked from your operation date; do the ones your physiotherapist has given you.

Listen to your knee. Mild discomfort is normal; sharp pain is not. Stop and contact your surgeon if you notice fever, calf pain, spreading numbness or wound drainage. This programme supports your physiotherapist's plan; it does not replace it.

Two layers on the same picture, never joined: the skeleton gives a whole-session knee angle, and repetitions are counted from movement in a box round the leg.
