Start and finish sessions

Start from your exercise position. Show an open palm towards the camera anywhere in the picture and hold it for two seconds. A five-second Marin countdown gives you time to lower your hand before recording starts. Your head does not need to be visible.

To finish, show an open palm for three seconds or select Finish. The app confirms when recording has stopped and you can move from your exercise position.

Spoken guidance uses Marin, an AI-generated voice with a soft, warm British female delivery. The countdown stays visible if audio is unavailable.

MediaPipe detects the hand gesture on this device. Camera access is needed; the microphone is not used.

Your programme, week by week

We are focusing first on heel slides, straight leg raises and seated knee extension, with accurate repetition counting and assessment of movement quality as our development priorities. After recording, movement analysis starts automatically. Your short summary shows repetitions, the main movement measurements, pace and time spent. You can also replay or download your video. Camera measurements and provisional SLR estimates are not validated clinical scores. Other exercises are shown as pending and will be introduced in due course. The week you are in now is marked from your operation date; do the ones your physiotherapist has given you.

Listen to your knee. Mild discomfort is normal; sharp pain is not. Stop and contact your surgeon if you notice fever, calf pain, spreading numbness or wound drainage. This programme supports your physiotherapist's plan; it does not replace it.

The skeleton shows visible joints and gives a whole-session knee angle. Camera repetitions require both optical movement and confirmation in your selected leg. Knee Recovery is a development prototype. Follow your physiotherapist’s or surgeon’s plan. The app does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

About the app and medical disclaimer

This prototype has not been clinically validated for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Camera estimates and repetition counts can be inaccurate or unavailable. They do not confirm that an exercise is safe or correctly performed, and provisional video estimates are not validated clinical scores.

Only perform exercises your treating clinician has advised. Do not change your rehabilitation plan on the basis of an app result. Stop exercising if you experience sharp pain and seek advice from your clinical team. The app is not an emergency service and sessions are not monitored by a clinician.

The exercise library and week labels provide a general structure, not an individual prescription. Some exercises remain pending while the app is developed.

Privacy and your data

Patient details, preferences, activity dates, exercise ticks, questionnaire reminders and results, and saved session results are stored in this browser on this device. They are not automatically sent to Cambridge Kinematics or your clinical team. There is no patient account or automatic synchronisation between devices. Clearing browser site data can remove these records, and other people using the same browser profile may be able to view them.

Camera frames are processed on this device. When enabled in Settings, exercise video can be recorded and session files downloaded. Downloaded files are separate from browser storage; keep them securely and share them only with people you choose.

Spoken prompts use generated Marin audio played by this device. The exercise flow does not request microphone access or listen for voice commands.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads fonts and, when needed, model resources from external providers. These services receive normal web-request information such as your IP address. Local processing does not mean that visiting the website makes no network requests.
