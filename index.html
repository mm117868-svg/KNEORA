Your details

Camera and options

The hand gesture is read from the pose model on this device. Voice control for start and finish uses the browser's own speech recognition, which may send the sound to Google or Apple while it listens, so it is off unless you turn it on.

Three ways to count, all of them on this device. Movement in the box counts from how the changed pixels move. Following the knee lays points on the knee and follows them from frame to frame, counting one repetition for each full swing; it holds up better when the movement is slow or small. Saying each number out loud puts the count in the patient's hands: say "one, two, three" and a small word spotter running on this device hears it, with the sound staying here. Whichever is chosen, movement in the box is still counted underneath as a cross-check, and both numbers go to the physiotherapist.

Your programme, week by week

We are focusing first on heel slides, straight leg raises, seated knee extension and standing knee bend, with accurate repetition counting and assessment of movement quality as our development priorities. Performance scoring is planned and is not yet available. Other exercises are shown as pending and will be introduced in due course. The week you are in now is marked from your operation date; do the ones your physiotherapist has given you.

Listen to your knee. Mild discomfort is normal; sharp pain is not. Stop and contact your surgeon if you notice fever, calf pain, spreading numbness or wound drainage. This programme supports your physiotherapist's plan; it does not replace it.
