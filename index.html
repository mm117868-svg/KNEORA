Your full programme, week by week

We are focusing first on heel slides, straight leg raises and seated knee extension, with accurate repetition counting and assessment of movement quality as our development priorities. Performance scoring is planned and is not yet available. Other exercises are shown as pending and will be introduced in due course. The week you are in now is marked from your operation date; do the ones your physiotherapist has given you.

Listen to your knee. Mild discomfort is normal; sharp pain is not. Stop and contact your surgeon if you notice fever, calf pain, spreading numbness or wound drainage. This programme supports your physiotherapist's plan; it does not replace it.
