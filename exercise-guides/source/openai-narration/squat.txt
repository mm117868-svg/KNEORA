Stand with feet hip width apart. Use a stable support if needed.

Keep your feet flat and your chest comfortably lifted. Look ahead.

Move your bottom backwards as you bend your hips and knees.

Keep your heels down and your knees aligned with your toes.

Push through both feet and straighten your hips and knees smoothly.

Go only as low as prescribed. Do not bounce or push through sharp pain.

Move back through your hips and bend both knees, keeping your feet planted.

Push through both feet and straighten your hips and knees smoothly.

Only progress to this exercise when your physiotherapist has recommended it.
