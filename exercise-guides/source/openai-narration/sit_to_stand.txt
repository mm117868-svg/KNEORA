Sit towards the front of a firm, stable chair. Place both feet flat.

Bring your feet back beneath your knees. Use the armrests if you need them.

Lean forwards from your hips, then push through your feet to stand.

Stand tall and steady. Use the armrests if you need support.

Move your bottom back and lower slowly onto the chair.

Keep your knees aligned with your toes. Avoid dropping into the seat.

Bring your chest forwards over your feet, then push up into standing.

Move your bottom back and lower slowly onto the chair.

Use the chair height and number of repetitions your physiotherapist recommends.
