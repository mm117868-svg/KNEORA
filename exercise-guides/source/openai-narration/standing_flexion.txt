Stand tall, holding a stable worktop for support.

Keep your feet about hip width apart and your knees facing forwards.

Bend your exercise knee and bring that heel up behind you.

Keep your thighs beside each other. Avoid leaning or arching your back.

Slowly lower your foot until it rests on the floor again.

Keep holding the support. Do not force the bend.

The thigh stays still as the heel rises behind you. Keep holding your support.

Slowly lower your foot until it rests on the floor again.

Only do this if prescribed. Use your own repetitions and holds.
