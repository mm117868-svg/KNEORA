Stand with feet hip width apart and hold a steady worktop.

Place your feet flat. Keep your knees pointing in the same direction as your toes.

Bend your hips and knees a little, moving your bottom backwards.

Keep both heels down, with your knees pointing in the same direction as your toes.

Press through your feet and slowly straighten your hips and knees.

Keep the bend shallow and comfortable. Avoid bouncing.

Move your bottom back and bend both knees a little. Keep your heels down.

Press through your feet and slowly straighten your hips and knees.

Only do this if prescribed. Follow your physiotherapist's range and repetitions.
