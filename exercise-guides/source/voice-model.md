# Exercise narration

All nine guides were generated using gpt-4o-mini-tts, voice Marin, with the saved soft, warm British delivery instructions. See openai-narration/render-manifest.json for actual cue durations and video timing changes. Narration is AI-generated.

Long speech cues are accommodated by extending the animation and its timing schedule together, without rushing or truncating the voice. Captions have been regenerated to match speech timestamps. Each staged video passed a decode check. Previous videos remain backed up in the local generation workspace. Subjective voice quality still benefits from a listening review.
