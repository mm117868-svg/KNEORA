Rest your head and shoulders. Keep the other leg relaxed on the bed.

Keep your hips still. Do not force the bend or push through sharp pain.

## Single-leg balance

Stand beside a stable worktop and keep a hand on it.

Stand tall with both feet flat. Look ahead and keep your support within reach.

Shift onto your operated leg. Lift the other foot just clear of the floor.

Keep your pelvis level. Look ahead and keep holding the support.

Gently return your lifted foot to the floor and rest.

Do not let go until your physiotherapist says you are ready.

Move your weight onto your operated leg, then lift the other foot a little.

Gently return your lifted foot to the floor and rest.

Use your prescribed hold time. Stop if you feel unsteady or dizzy.
