# Recorded exercise analyser

Serve the parent directory locally with `python3 -m http.server 8766 --bind 127.0.0.1` and open http://localhost:8766/video-analysis/ . Select a local MP4 or WebM recording. Video stays in the browser; JavaScript, WASM and model assets are served from the bundled vendor directory. No external model download is needed. Standalone uploads are not stored automatically. In the main app, completed analyses are saved as compact session measurements; videos remain temporary unless downloaded.

Choose the anatomical leg, analysis start time and optional prescribed targets. Leave targets blank for measurements without a score. No initial pause is required. The analyser estimates the lowered position from tracked frames anywhere after the selected start time. Include a return to the lowered position. A clip that starts mid-lift can still be used; its opening partial repetition is not scored. Film one person lying down, squarely from the side, with shoulder, hip, knee and ankle visible. Supported duration: 1 to 300 seconds. Processing samples at 10 Hz, at up to 960 pixels width, using MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Full in image mode. This deliberately avoids video-tracker state leaking between seeks or repeated analyses.

Geometry uses pixel aspect ratio and estimates knee bend as 180 minus the hip-knee-ankle angle. Lift is the reduction in shoulder-hip-knee angle relative to an automatically inferred lowered-position reference. It is a 2D proxy affected by trunk movement and perspective, not a calibrated 3D hip angle. Camera or trunk movement can invalidate the result without being detected. Supine tracking remains to be validated. Presence of more than one detected person rejects the frame.

Prototype rules: landmarks visibility >= 0.5 by default; reference uses three-frame rolling medians without crossing tracking gaps longer than 0.25 s, then takes the median of candidates within 3 degrees of the largest observed hip angle; at least one three-frame candidate window is required; lift starts at 6 degrees and ends at <= 3 degrees; complete cycles 0.8 to 30 seconds. Missing tracking interrupts a cycle; a new resting observation is required before counting again. Score is a count of selected criteria met (plus knee control when any target is supplied), not a validated clinical scale. Range tolerance is 3 degrees; timing tolerance 0.15 seconds; knee bending must exceed the entered tolerance continuously for >=0.3 seconds. Hold is longest continuous time in the target zone, or within 3 degrees of the observed peak when no lift target is supplied. Lowering starts after the peak plateau. Very slow initial movement can bias phase timing. Out-of-plane motion, small knee changes, occlusion and loose clothing require separate validation.

No muscle strength, pain, passive extension, diagnosis or autonomous exercise progression is inferred. No clinician training dataset is used. Feedback describes observations and prompts review of knee-control problems. The reference can itself contain limited extension; this is reported separately. It is the lowest observed leg position, not proof of true rest. A clip that never fully lowers the leg may bias the reference and reported range.

Tests: run `node --test video-analysis/analysis.test.mjs` from the parent directory. Synthetic tests verify geometry, segmentation, dropouts and criteria. They do not establish real-video or clinical accuracy.

References: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31217121/ . This implementation reuses the existing application's MediaPipe approach; it does not embed Sports2D or the KIMORE data.

## End-of-video report (version 4)

Every completed processing run now produces a report, including when no full repetitions or no baseline are available. Knee and hip measurements are accepted independently where their required landmarks meet the existing visibility rules. Reported minimum, maximum, observed range, mean, median, peak timestamp and per-metric frame coverage describe only observed frames. Approximate hip flexion is 180 degrees minus the shoulder-hip-knee included angle. It is a 2D trunk-relative proxy, not calibrated anatomical hip flexion. Partial hip observations can contribute to the lift trace once a reference is available, but full tracking is still required to assess repetitions. Null means unavailable, never zero.

Downloads: human-readable TXT report, numeric frame CSV and full JSON including settings, summaries and per-repetition data. Tracking visibility is not measurement accuracy. Timing averages are unavailable when no full repetitions are assessed.

## MediaPipe frame quality (version 6)

Frames are classified as accepted, partial or rejected. Required landmarks must have visibility >=0.5 by default and, when supplied by the runtime, presence >=0.5, finite coordinates inside the image and adjacent segments >=20 pixels at processing resolution. No detection or multiple detected people rejects the frame. Missing shoulder data can preserve knee measurements, and missing ankle data can preserve hip measurements. Rejected values remain null and cannot enter joint statistics. SLR repetition scoring requires both joints; heel-slide and seated-extension cycles require the knee only; tracking gaps interrupt the repetition. Reports include reason counts and CSV/JSON records retain frame status and reasons. Thresholds are engineering checks and cannot guarantee that all bad poses are detected. This is not a blur detector or validated measurement-quality classifier.

## Adjustable visibility threshold (version 7)

Landmark visibility now defaults to 50%, reduced from 70%. The optional settings allow 10–95% in 5-point steps. The selected value is recorded in the report and full JSON. Presence remains at 50% when provided by the model; image bounds and segment-size checks remain active. Lower visibility accepts less certain landmark estimates and can increase errors. Existing reports are not changed; rerun analysis to apply a new threshold.

## Final SLR assessment (version 8)

The final panel separates an automatic exploratory video estimate from a clinician-entered SLR component score. At least 80% full frame coverage, an inferred reference and a complete detected lift are needed for a video estimate. These are engineering gates, not validated clinical criteria. The prototype estimates 2 if any complete trial stays within the selected maximum additional bend tolerance and otherwise estimates 1. Its best-trial selection and degree tolerance are not specified by the paper. It never automatically assigns 0 based on absent detections. This adaptation cannot establish heel clearance, the study's 2-foot target or full available knee extension; the clinical score must be assessed using Figure 1 of Bade et al. (2018), doi:10.1016/j.apmr.2017.07.013. Clinical component entries 0–2 are retained separately and may be entered even when camera tracking is inadequate. No clinical efficacy or validated automated scoring claim is made. TXT and JSON include both provenance and reasons.

## Live recording and three exercises (version 9)

The main patient app now records a temporary video for analysis whenever MediaRecorder is available. After Stop finishes writing the final video chunk, the session summary shows **Analyse exercise**. The button opens the analyser with that recording, exercise, leg preference, duration and postoperative day already supplied. No upload or repeated file selection is required. Recorded clips must be 1 to 300 seconds long. Saving the video to Downloads is a separate option; the in-memory recording is released when leaving the session. The analysis summary is saved into the session's `exercise_analysis` field; it has no video or raw frame trace. It can be downloaded again from the patient's progress history. The analyser offers full TXT, CSV and JSON downloads before leaving the session.

`exercises.mjs` adds heel slides and seated knee extension. Their segmentation uses knee measurements even if the shoulder is missing. A rolling three-frame median locates the least bent reference for heel slides and the most bent reference for seated extension. Cycles move at least 6 degrees away, reach at least 10 degrees of excursion, and return within 3 degrees; durations are 0.8 to 30 seconds. These are engineering thresholds, not clinical cut-offs. Missing frames or timestamp gaps over 0.25 seconds interrupt cycles. No fixed starting pause is required. Auto leg selection chooses the most measurable anatomical leg once for the whole recording; the patient should confirm the intended leg. Explicit left/right selection is respected.

Reports include all available joint summaries, per-repetition knee extremes, hip estimates, motion excursion, cycle duration, outward/return time, hold, cadence and coverage. Non-SLR movements have no fabricated clinical or technique score. Clinical context uses the operation day and the linked evidence in [POSTOPERATIVE_EVIDENCE.md](POSTOPERATIVE_EVIDENCE.md), without day-specific pass/fail targets or synthetic percentiles.

Run `node --test video-analysis/analysis.test.mjs video-analysis/exercises.test.mjs`. The local `video-analysis/tests/live-integration.html` checks MediaRecorder finalisation, embedded handoff, report return and no-person output using a generated canvas recording. It also loads the existing seated-extension example for browser/model checks. This is software verification, not clinical validation. Preserve these files, imports and the post-recording integration when regenerating the main page from an upstream generator.
