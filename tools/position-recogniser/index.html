Straight-leg raise position recogniser

Recognises resting, intermediate and raised positions from each image. It uses the starting image as a reference and does not follow persistent tracking points.

Research prototype. Trained on one recording of one person. It needs a similar side-on view of the lower limbs. Other people, clothing, backgrounds and tiny movements have not been validated. It cannot yet reliably distinguish which leg moved. No patient session is saved here.
