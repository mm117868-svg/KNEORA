What this example shows

The five recorded movements are counted once each, including movements held before returning. The fixed box marks the image area being analysed. This method uses classical image comparison without a pose model.

This is a recorded replay using a clip inspected during development. It does not establish accuracy in other homes, with a live camera, or at a measured distance of 3–5 metres. It does not measure knee angles or exercise quality.

Audio is omitted. The final frame is held for two seconds so the completed count can be read.
