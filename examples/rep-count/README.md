# Repetition-count video exemplar

[![Watch the seated knee-extension repetition-count exemplar](poster.jpg)](https://mm117868-svg.github.io/knee-recovery/examples/rep-count/)

[Watch the video](https://mm117868-svg.github.io/knee-recovery/examples/rep-count/) · [Download the MP4](seated-extension-rep-count.mp4) · [Replay events](replay-events.json)

This 41-second exemplar shows five seated knee extensions with holds. The counter advances from zero to five at the event times measured by the experimental return-to-start counter in browser playback. The image-comparison method does not use a pose model.

The recording was inspected during development. This is an example of observed behaviour, not independent accuracy validation, a live-camera test, or evidence of performance at a measured 3–5 metre distance. It does not assess knee angles or exercise quality. The other counting modes in the patient app use different methods.

The full source recording plays at normal speed in a side-by-side presentation. Audio and source metadata are omitted. The last video frame is held for two seconds to display the final count. The count overlay is rendered from the saved replay events; it is not being calculated by the video player. The original source filename was `WhatsApp Video 2026-09-13 at 02.04.36.mp4`.
